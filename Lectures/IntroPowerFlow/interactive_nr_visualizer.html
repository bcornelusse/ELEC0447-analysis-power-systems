Quadratic Convergence Behavior

Solving: g(x) = 4 - x³ = 0 &implies; x* = 4^(1/3) ≈ 1.587401
Newton Step: Δx = (c - f(x)) / f'(x) = (4 - x³) / (3x²)

Notice how the error squares at each iteration near the root: if the error is 10⁻¹, the next error is ~10⁻², then ~10⁻⁴, then ~10⁻⁸. This is the fundamental reason why power flow solvers converge in only 3 to 5 iterations!
